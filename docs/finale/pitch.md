# Chameleon — pitch draft

Every campaign has to live on many canvases. Designers repeatedly rebuild the same idea for posters, social posts, stories, event headers, and banners.

Traditional resize tools adjust geometry. Chameleon reads the relationships inside a design—its headline, hero image, supporting copy, brand marks, and actions—then recomposes those pieces for a new format.

One campaign becomes a coordinated family. Designers can preview the alternatives, keep a key element in place, and apply a selected format as a new Adobe Express page.

The Stage 1 engine is deterministic and runs offline. On the three supplied designs and the documented synthetic suites, it preserves the required source content and passes the recorded structural, clipping, image-ratio, collision, and determinism checks. Those proxy and synthetic results are evidence about this implementation, not Adobe's hidden score or proof of generalization to every design.

## One-line version

**Chameleon keeps the design idea intact while rebuilding its layout for every canvas.**
